Stream Language

Many channels exist in several language editions (for example ESPN, ESPN Latino and ESPN Brasil). Pick the languages you watch, in order. Prefer ranks those streams first and keeps others only as a last resort so the channel still plays; Only never uses a stream known to be in another language. Streams that do not say their language are always kept. Saving re-searches the 24/7 channels.
