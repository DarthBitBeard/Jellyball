Settings → "Channels Between Games" chooses when team channels appear in
Jellyfin's channel list: always (as before), on game day, or around the game,
from 15, 30 or 60 minutes before the start (sooner when a pregame stream is
found) until the game is over and its stream has stopped. Channels being
watched, or with a recording or series recording set up in Jellyfin, always
stay listed; always-live and Multi-View channels, and channels without a known
schedule, are not affected. Jellyball refreshes Jellyfin's guide as channels
come and go, within a minute when one appears. The guide keeps every channel's
programmes either way, because Jellyfin reuses a downloaded guide for about an
hour.
