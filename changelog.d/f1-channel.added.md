A **Formula 1 channel**, in a new opt-in **Motorsport** group: turn it on under
"More leagues & channel groups (opt-in)", then enable Formula 1 in the catalog.
It follows every session of every race weekend (practice, sprint qualifying,
sprint, qualifying, race) from ESPN's schedule, searches only around sessions,
and matches the current round's F1 event while ignoring F2, F3, F1 Academy,
MotoGP and the next round's listings. In the guide each session is its own
programme ("F1 Qualifying", episode "São Paulo GP"), so Jellyfin's "Record
Series" on "F1 Race" records every race. The channel leaves the guide between
seasons like the team channels.
